## Namespaces

### margin
- **Purpose**: Margin call calculation and lifecycle for all product lines.
- **Workloads**: `margin-calc`, `margin-lifecycle`, `margin-notifier`, `margin-recon-job` (CronJob)
- **Repo(s)**: `collateral/margin-service`, `collateral/margin-notifier`
- **Owner**: margin squad
- **Identity**: SA `margin-sa` -> workload identity -> client `b24e8f17-...-9a03` -> reads `kv-collateral-uat` (secrets get), Sybase UAT read-only account, blob `sauatmargincalc` (read/write). No expiry (federated).
- **Care needed**:
  - `margin-lifecycle` is a singleton consumer. Two replicas double-process margin calls. Never scale above 1.
  - `margin-recon-job` runs 18:30 SGT. Restarting mid-run duplicates recon entries; wait for completion.
- **Gotchas**: `margin-calc` holds a warm eligibility cache. Restarting it alone leaves `margin-lifecycle` serving stale eligibility for ~5 minutes.

### collateral-api
- **Purpose**: External-facing REST API for collateral positions and agreements (UAT stub of the prod surface).
- **Workloads**: `collateral-api`, `collateral-api-migrator` (Job, runs on deploy)
- **Repo(s)**: `collateral/collateral-api`
- **Owner**: platform squad
- **Identity**: SA `collateral-api-sa` -> SP with client secret -> client `3d9a7c02-...-41bf` -> `kv-collateral-uat` (secrets get), Sybase UAT read-only. **Secret expires 2026-11-30** - owner: platform squad.
- **Care needed**: `collateral-api-migrator` must finish before `collateral-api` becomes ready. A failed migrator leaves the API crash-looping on a schema it does not have.
- **Gotchas**: Still on the SP-secret mechanism, unlike the other namespaces. Auth failures here look different - check expiry first.

### pricing
- **Purpose**: Price sourcing and valuation. Internal pricer plus the Reuters feed adapter.
- **Workloads**: `pricing-service`, `reuters-adapter`, `price-cache` (StatefulSet)
- **Repo(s)**: `collateral/pricing-service`, `collateral/reuters-adapter`
- **Owner**: pricing squad
- **Identity**: SA `pricing-sa` -> workload identity -> client `91c4b6de-...-7e22` -> `kv-pricing-uat` (secrets get - holds the Reuters credential), Sybase UAT read-only.
- **Care needed**:
  - `price-cache` is a StatefulSet with a PVC. Deleting a pod is fine; deleting the PVC loses the warm cache and forces a full reload (~20 min).
  - `pricing-service` must be up before `margin-calc`, or margin falls back to stale prices without erroring.
- **Gotchas**: `reuters-adapter` is quiet outside market hours. Zero throughput at 03:00 SGT is normal, not a fault.

### ops-ui
- **Purpose**: React console ops use for the UAT sign-off cycle.
- **Workloads**: `ops-ui`, `ops-ui-bff`
- **Repo(s)**: `collateral/ops-ui`
- **Owner**: ops tooling squad
- **Identity**: SA `ops-ui-bff-sa` -> workload identity -> client `5a0f32bb-...-cc71` -> calls `collateral-api` and `margin-calc` in-cluster. No direct Azure resource access.
- **Care needed**: none.
- **Gotchas**: Served through the shared ingress below, not its own. A 404 here is usually an ingress rule, not the pod.

## Cluster-level notes
- **Ingress**: single shared nginx ingress in `ingress-nginx`, owned by the platform team. Host `collateral-uat.internal.<org>`. Path-based routing - a 404 on a known service is usually the ingress rule, not the workload.
- **Shared infrastructure**: `ingress-nginx` and `monitoring` are platform-owned. Findings there get reported to platform, not acted on.
- **Node pools**: `systempool` (platform), `apppool` (everything above), `batchpool` (tainted `workload=batch:NoSchedule` - only `margin-recon-job` tolerates it). A batch pod stuck Pending is usually a missing toleration.
- **Differs from siblings**: this is the only cluster where ops have namespace read access, so unexpected `kubectl` activity in `margin` may be theirs.
